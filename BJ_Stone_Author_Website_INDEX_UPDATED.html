MEET THE AUTHOR

A life lived close to the land

BJ Stone is a Texas author, lifelong horsewoman, mule rider, former professional trainer and clinician, and ranch woman whose books are rooted in the country she knows firsthand. Her published work spans the Shadow Valley Western Series, contemporary Western fiction, psychological suspense, science fiction, Texas ranch history, mule-riding instruction, and cowboy faith.

Her stories carry the rhythms of horses, mules, ranch work, family, hard choices, and the landscapes that shape the people who live there.
